OpenRLink EV diagnostic profiles use protocol mappings and conversion formulas
from the Open Vehicle Monitoring System (OVMS). Sources are linked in
docs/OpenRLink-1.5-EV.md. Kotlin implementations are maintained by OpenRLink.
